Silent Endometriosis: The Unseen Cause of Unexplained Infertility

By Dr. Santosh Gupta October 26, 2026

When most women think of endometriosis, they envision debilitating menstrual cramps, severe pelvic pain, and heavy periods that interrupt daily life. However, one of the most perplexing findings in reproductive medicine is that up to 20% to 25% of women with endometriosis have zero pain symptoms.

These women often present with a diagnosis of 'Unexplained Infertility'—their periods are regular, their hormone levels appear normal, and standard pelvic ultrasounds show no obvious cysts. Yet, month after month, pregnancy does not occur.

In my practice consulting at Nova IVF Fertility in Koramangala and Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru, diagnosing and treating silent endometriosis has unlocked successful pregnancies for hundreds of couples who had previously spent years pursuing unsuccessful fertility attempts.

What Is Silent Endometriosis?

Endometriosis occurs when tissue resembling the endometrial lining of the uterus implants outside the uterine cavity—on the ovaries, fallopian tubes, pelvic peritoneum, bladder, and bowel.

In silent endometriosis, the lesions do not necessarily infiltrate nerve-rich pelvic tissue to cause acute pain, but they produce intense localized chronic inflammation. This biochemical storm impairs fertility in subtle yet profound ways.

How Asymptomatic Endometriosis Blocks Conception

1. Toxic Peritoneal Micro-Environment

Endometriotic implants release inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-1, IL-6) and reactive oxygen species into the pelvic peritoneal fluid. This hostile environment impairs sperm motility, damages sperm DNA, and hinders egg-sperm binding.

2. Compromised Oocyte & Embryo Quality

Chronic ovarian inflammation diminishes mitochondrial function inside developing oocytes, resulting in fragile eggs, lower fertilization rates, and poor Day 5 blastocyst formation.

3. Impaired Endometrial Receptivity (Progesterone Resistance)

Endometriosis triggers systemic progesterone resistance inside the uterine cavity. The endometrium fails to transform properly during the implantation window, preventing healthy blastocyst attachment.

4. Pelvic Adhesions & Tubal Distortion

Microscopic inflammatory exudate causes delicate pelvic organs to adhere together, altering the anatomical relationship between the fallopian tube fimbriae and the ovary, preventing natural egg pick-up.

How Is Silent Endometriosis Diagnosed?

Because standard 2D pelvic ultrasound cannot detect superficial peritoneal endometriosis implants or subtle tubal adhesions, advanced diagnostic tools are essential:

• High-Resolution 3D Pelvic Ultrasound by an Expert: Evaluates soft markers like the 'sliding sign' of the uterus against the rectum, subtle ovarian immobility, and deep infiltrating nodules.
• The BCL6 / ReceptivaDX Biopsy: A specialized endometrial biopsy detecting the BCL6 biomarker, a protein strongly overexpressed when silent pelvic endometriosis is present.
• Diagnostic Laparoscopy: The definitive gold-standard procedure allowing direct microscopic visualization and biopsy of suspicious peritoneal lesions.

Tailored Treatment Strategies at Our Bengaluru Clinics

  • Conservative Laparoscopic Excision: For young women seeking natural conception or prior to IVF, precise surgical excision of endometriotic implants restores normal pelvic anatomy and alleviates localized inflammation.
  • Long Down-Regulation Protocol (GnRH Agonist Suppression): Prior to a Frozen Embryo Transfer, administering 1 to 3 months of Depot GnRH agonists (such as Leuprolide) induces temporary hormonal down-regulation. This starves endometriotic implants, clears pelvic inflammation, and resets endometrial progesterone receptivity.
  • Freeze-All Strategy with Blastocyst Culture: Avoiding fresh embryo transfer protects embryos from ovarian hyper-stimulation inflammatory cytokines. Embryos are grown to Day 5 blastocysts, vitrified, and transferred into a suppressed, quiet uterine environment.
  • Anti-Inflammatory Nutraceutical Support: Supplementation with high-potency Curcumin, Resveratrol, N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC), and Omega-3 fatty acids helps quench pelvic oxidative stress.

Takeaway for Unexplained Infertility Patients

'Unexplained Infertility' simply means that standard basic tests have not yet revealed the answer. If you have been trying to conceive without success despite normal initial results, investigating silent endometriosis can transform an unexplained hurdle into a clear, treatable roadmap.
