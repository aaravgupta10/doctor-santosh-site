Diagnostic Forensics

What Dr. Santosh Evaluates in Your Previous Records

Too many couples are subjected to the exact same standard IVF cycle repeated 2 or 3 times. We look deeper to find what other clinics missed.

1. Stimulation Protocols

Were your gonadotropin dosages too high (damaging egg quality) or too low? Was the trigger shot given at the exact peak follicular size?

Checks: Dose, Trigger, Follicle Sync

2. Embryology Quality

Did embryos arrest on Day 3 or Day 5? Were blastocysts graded accurately? Day 3 transfers often mask lab quality issues that Blastocyst culture resolves.

Checks: Day 3 vs 5, Blastocyst Grading

3. Sperm DNA & Genetics

Standard semen tests miss high sperm DNA fragmentation (DFI). When DFI exceeds 25%, ICSI or IMSI protocols are required to select healthy sperm.

Checks: Sperm DFI, PGT-A Aneuploidy

4. Uterine Cavity & Lining

Was there a thin endometrium (< 7mm)? Undetected polyps, adhesions, or chronic endometritis? A gentle hysteroscopy often corrects this.

Checks: Lining thickness, Hysteroscopy
Preparation Checklist

What Reports Should You Bring to Your Second Opinion?

You are legally entitled to your full medical and embryology lab records from any clinic in India under the ART Act 2021. Gathering these reports gives Dr. Santosh the full picture.

Don't have all reports? Don't worry. Bring whatever discharge summaries, ultrasound scans, or blood work you currently possess.
  • Previous IVF Stimulation Sheet: Daily dosages of injections (rFSH, hMG), days of stimulation, and trigger medication used.
  • Embryology Development Summary: Number of eggs retrieved, mature eggs (MII), fertilization rate, and Day 3 / Day 5 blastocyst grades.
  • Endometrial Preparation Details: Progesterone protocols used before embryo transfer and lining thickness measurements.
  • Semen Analysis & DNA Fragmentation: Both basic counts and DFI percentages if tested previously.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Dr. Santosh force me to repeat all tests?

No. Dr. Santosh believes strongly in ethical medical practice. If your previous ultrasound scans, blood tests, or genetic screenings were conducted within acceptable clinical timelines by accredited laboratories, she will review those existing records without forcing unnecessary, expensive re-testing.

Can I do an online Second Opinion if I live outside Bengaluru?

Yes. While an in-person physical consultation at Koramangala or Bannerghatta Road is optimal for detailed ultrasound examination, couples from outside Bengaluru or international patients can share their reports via WhatsApp / email for a scheduled tele-consultation.
