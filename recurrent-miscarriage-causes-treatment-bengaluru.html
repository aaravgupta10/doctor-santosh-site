Recurrent Pregnancy Loss: Root Causes, Diagnostic Workup & Proven Solutions

By Dr. Santosh Gupta October 23, 2026

A positive pregnancy test brings immense joy and hope. When that joy is cut short by a miscarriage, the grief is profound. Experiencing two or more consecutive pregnancy losses—clinically termed Recurrent Pregnancy Loss (RPL)—is an agonizing journey that leaves couples feeling overwhelmed and seeking clear answers.

One of the greatest misconceptions patients carry is the belief that they did something wrong—exercised too much, experienced too much stress, or ate the wrong food. In reality, early pregnancy loss is overwhelmingly driven by biological, chromosomal, anatomical, or immunological factors completely outside the mother's control.

Over 25+ years of specialized reproductive practice in Bengaluru, I have had the privilege of guiding countless couples with 3, 4, or even 5 prior losses to the birth of a healthy baby. With methodical, multi-factorial diagnostics, over 70% to 75% of couples with recurrent loss achieve a successful live birth.

When Is a Recurrent Loss Workup Recommended?

Historically, medical textbooks required couples to suffer three consecutive miscarriages before initiating a comprehensive investigation. Today, international fertility societies and our clinical practice recommend a thorough workup after two consecutive clinical pregnancy losses, particularly if the woman is 35 or older.

The Primary Causes of Recurrent Pregnancy Loss

1. Chromosomal & Genetic Anomalies (50% - 60%)

The single most common cause of early first-trimester loss is embryonic aneuploidy (missing or extra chromosomes). Furthermore, in 3% to 5% of couples, one partner carries a balanced parental reciprocal or Robertsonian translocation, producing unbalanced gametes that result in miscarriage.

2. Antiphospholipid Syndrome (APS) & Thrombophilia

An acquired autoimmune condition where maternal antibodies attack phospholipids, causing micro-thrombi (clots) in the delicate placental vasculature, starving the fetus of oxygen. Inherited thrombophilias (Factor V Leiden, Prothrombin mutations) can also impair implantation.

3. Uterine Cavity & Anatomical Defects

Congenital uterine malformations—most notably a uterine septum (a fibrous band dividing the cavity with poor vascularity)—are major contributors to recurrent loss. Submucosal fibroids, large endometrial polyps, and Asherman's syndrome also prevent healthy placental attachment.

4. Endocrine & Hormonal Imbalances

Uncontrolled thyroid disease (elevated TSH or anti-TPO antibodies), elevated prolactin, unmanaged diabetes with elevated HbA1c, and severe Luteal Phase Deficiency (inadequate progesterone production) disrupt early gestational support.

The Comprehensive Diagnostic Roadmap

  • Parental Karyotyping: Chromosomal blood analysis of both partners to detect balanced reciprocal or Robertsonian translocations.
  • Autoimmune & Thrombophilia Screening: Testing for Lupus Anticoagulant, Anticardiolipin Antibodies (IgG/IgM), Anti-Beta-2 Glycoprotein 1, and hereditary clotting factors.
  • 3D Pelvic Saline Infusion Sonohysterography / Diagnostic Hysteroscopy: Provides gold-standard visualization of the uterine cavity to detect septums, polyps, or adhesions.
  • Complete Endocrine Panel: Fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, high-sensitivity TSH, Free T4, anti-TPO antibodies, and serum Prolactin.
  • Sperm DNA Fragmentation Index (DFI): High paternal sperm DNA damage is an established, treatable trigger of early embryonic genetic instability and miscarriage.

Evidence-Based Solutions for Recurrent Loss

Once the underlying mechanism is diagnosed, treatment is targeted and highly effective:

• For Uterine Septums: Minimally invasive hysteroscopic metroplasty safely resects the fibrous septum, restoring normal cavity shape and dramatically lowering miscarriage rates to near-normal levels.
• For Antiphospholipid Syndrome (APS): Combination therapy with low-dose aspirin (75–150 mg) and prophylactic low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) injections from the moment pregnancy is confirmed increases live birth rates to over 80%.
• For Age-Related Aneuploidy & Translocations: IVF combined with PGT-A (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidies) allows us to genetically screen embryos before transfer, selecting only euploid (chromosomally normal) blastocysts and eliminating the risk of aneuploid miscarriage.
• Optimized Progesterone Support: Micronized vaginal and injectable progesterone maintains early decidual stability throughout the critical first 10 to 12 weeks of pregnancy.

A Message of Hope

Recurrent pregnancy loss is a medical condition, not a personal failing. With precision diagnostic investigation and individualized medical protocols, the majority of couples can fulfill their dream of bringing home a healthy baby.
